# SPELLCHECK pitch

## 30-second pitch

> “SPELLCHECK is a keyboard-first fantasy defense game where the battle chooses the spell, but the player must type it under pressure. Correct casts change the world and force a tactical rerank. Typos become deterministic cursed magic, and an event-aware bard remembers the mistake and recovery. Agents authored and validated the spell corpus, deterministic ranker, and event-aware bard, plus a bundled local voice pack with browser fallback; this build needs no runtime model.”

## One-sentence close

> “SPELLCHECK turns combat into language, human mistakes into magic, and one unreliable hero into a story worth retelling.”
